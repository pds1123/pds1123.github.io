Civic data · Geospatial reconciliation

NewStart NZ

An Auckland housing and safety guide that lets newcomers weigh rent against crime data when choosing where to live.

MBIE and NZ Police publish useful data, but their geographic boundaries do not match each other or the suburb names people use. I built a reconciliation layer that folds both sources into one model, then made the results explorable on a map.
